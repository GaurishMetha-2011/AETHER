# AETHER

**A window that could make electricity.**

AETHER is a student project by **Gaurish Metha** at **Indian School Muscat**. It explores a simple idea: could a clear window also help make power from sunlight?

## The idea

Most of the window would stay clear. A thin layer could catch some sunlight and guide light through the glass. Small solar cells around the edge could turn that light into electricity. Wires would carry the power away.

```text
Sunlight → light-catching layer → light travels through glass
         → solar cells at the edge → electricity
```

AETHER is not meant to replace roof solar panels. It asks whether windows could become another place to explore solar power.

## Materials

The project is exploring tiny carbon-based materials called **Carbon Quantum Dots (CQDs)** for the light-catching layer. Farm waste is one possible starting point to study. The material, the way to make it, and whether it works in clear glass all still need to be tested.

## Where the project stands

AETHER is a design and research project moving toward a small physical test window. The window has not yet been built or tested. The website's diagrams, power example, and future-building images are illustrations, not experimental results or real installations.

The next goal is to test each step: does the layer catch light, does light reach the edge, and can the solar cells make electricity from it?

## In the news

AETHER was featured online and in the newspaper by **Muscat Daily** on 27 September 2026. The article discusses project goals; the window has not yet been built or tested, so its figures are not measured results.

## About

AETHER brings together ideas about clear windows, light, new materials, solar cells and buildings. The long-term goal is to learn whether these ideas can work together in a real window.
