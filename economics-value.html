Why the extra cost?

A window with
more to offer.

The extra cost is meant to pay for a window that could also make electricity and help keep buildings cool.

01 / SOLAR CELLS AT THE EDGE

Keep more glass clear.

The idea is to guide sunlight through the window to small solar cells around its edges, instead of covering the whole window with dark panels.

The cost estimate allows for more edge-cell area than the early 4% idea. The window’s final look will depend on its design.
02 / MAKING USE OF WASTE

Turn leftovers into useful material.

AETHER explores making its light-catching material from carbon-based ingredients such as citric acid and urea. Some of these ingredients can come from farm or food waste.

The aim is to find a simpler, lower-cost way to make the material for a whole window.
