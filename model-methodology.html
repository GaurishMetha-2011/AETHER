The illustrative model

How we got
this estimate.

The calculator multiplies a fixed set of illustrative optical and electrical assumptions by the selected sunlight intensity and daily full-power sun hours. These values are editable in the site code, not experimental AETHER material properties.

The formula

ηtotal = fUV × fabs × ηPL × (λUV / λem) × ηtrap × ηguide × ηedge × ηPV

Both wavelengths are converted from nanometres to metres before calculating their ratio: (350 × 10−9 m) / (690 × 10−9 m). The wavelength ratio accounts for the energy of a photon changing when UV light is re-emitted as lower-energy visible light.

ηtotal = 0.04 × 0.50 × 0.70 × (350 / 690) × 0.75 × 0.70 × 0.80 × 0.25
ηtotal ≈ 0.0007456522 = 0.07456522%
