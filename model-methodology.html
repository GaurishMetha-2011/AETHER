The illustrative model

How we got
this estimate.

The simulator compares two overall-efficiency scenarios. They come from different research contexts and are not measured performance of the AETHER window.

Window & scenario assumptions

Model parameterValue and context
Window width1.2 m
Window height1.5 m
Fixed window area1.2 m × 1.5 m = 1.8 m²
Research benchmark1.57% (η = 0.0157)Literature-reported efficiency for a particular quantum-dot glass device; not demonstrated for AETHER.
Ambitious design target4.5% (η = 0.045)Projected target for certain commercial-scale semi-transparent luminescent solar concentrator designs; not demonstrated for AETHER.

How the estimate is calculated

area = window width × window height = 1.2 m × 1.5 m = 1.8 m²
incident power = area × solar irradiance
electrical power = incident power × selected overall efficiency
daily energy (Wh/day) = electrical power × equivalent full-power sunlight hours
daily energy (kWh/day) = daily energy (Wh/day) / 1,000

The selected scenario already represents an overall system efficiency. The calculator applies it directly and does not multiply by component efficiencies again, which would count losses twice. Irradiance means solar power per square metre incident on the window surface; no additional angle correction is applied.
